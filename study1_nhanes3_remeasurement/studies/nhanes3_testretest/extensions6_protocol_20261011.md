# Post hoc extension protocol 6 (E12): analyses prompted by the independent referee review of revision 7, draft 1

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: post hoc, protocol saved before any E12 quantity was computed (SHA-256 of this file recorded in the results file). Data: Study 1 (NHANES III reexamination), frozen participant export `analysis_v1/stage3/stage3_participants.csv`, `nh3_build.build()` frames, frozen cluster-bootstrap weights (`S.boot_weights(cluster, 4000, 20261009)`), and the E11 cached uniform-start bootstrap fits (`analysis_v1_posthoc/_cache_e11`). Script: `scripts/nh3_ext6_review.py` → `analysis_v1_posthoc/ext6_review_results.json`.

Origin. An independent referee-style review of the revision-7 draft (2026-10-11; `manuscript_v7/review_referee_v7_draft1.md`) asked for (M1b) a per-zone denominator for the net-benefit loss, (M1d) an exchange-rate sensitivity, (M3) uncertainty for the instability-zone boundaries, (M5) checks of the reexamination subsample (interval between examinations; completeness), and (M2) a model-based transport of the error variance to modern measurement conditions. All four were judged cheap and decision-relevant; none changes a prespecified estimand. The referee's reading of the results was known when this protocol was written; the quantities below had not been computed.

## A. Uncertainty of the instability zones and peaks

1. Rebuild the prespecified π_clin model (K3 'clin', base PREVENT-ASCVD) and its 4,000 frozen cluster-bootstrap refits exactly as `nh3_reclassification_lookup.refit` does (assertion of exact reproduction of the frozen knots, coefficients, curve values and intervals).
2. For every replicate and each threshold (3%, 5%, 10%), apply the frozen zone rule: on the fine grid of logit distance d ∈ [0, 1.5] (1,501 points), the zone exists only if π(d = 0) ≥ level; d* is the last grid point before π first falls below the level (or 1.5 if it never does); limits are expit(logit t ∓ d*). Levels 0.25 and 0.10. Truncate limits at the logit midpoints between thresholds, as in the repeat-strategy and E11 zone definitions (`nh3_ext2021_burden.zones`).
3. Report, per threshold and level: the point zone (must equal the frozen zone after truncation), 95% percentile intervals of the lower limit, the upper limit and the width (percentage points and logit units), and the share of replicates in which the zone is empty. Report the peak π(d = 0) with its interval (already frozen; recomputed as a check).
4. Report the share of participants whose first-visit risk lies in the union of the ≥25% zones, with an interval that recomputes the zones in each replicate and weights participants by the replicate weights (zone and sampling uncertainty together), beside the fixed-zone share.
5. The same for the post hoc 7.5% total-CVD crossing curve (`nh3_posthoc_cvd75_curve.py` specification; single threshold, no truncation), after asserting reproduction of its frozen coefficients and zones.
6. Reporting rule for the manuscript: give zone limits to one decimal with their intervals in the Supplement; in the main text round each limit to the nearest 0.5 pp when its interval half-width exceeds 0.5 pp, and present the three peaks as a range rather than as three distinct values.

## B. Net benefit at stake: per-zone denominator and exchange-rate sensitivity

Populations, thresholds, zones and model exactly as E11 (`nh3_ext5_stakes.py`): base 10-year ASCVD at 3%, 5%, 10% in statin-naive participants (n = 749); 10-year total CVD at 7.5% in participants not taking antihypertensive medication (n = 623); 30-year ASCVD at 10% at ages 30–59 years (n = 499). Usual-level distribution refitted with the E11 point-estimate procedure (`fit_g`, identical arguments, so identical to E11); intervals from the E11 cached 1,000 uniform-start replicates, each evaluated with the E11 replicate Monte Carlo size (20,000 draws, seed 20261011 + b).

1. Per-zone denominator. With the E11 Monte Carlo draws (200,000; same seed and draw order as `metrics`), the first-visit estimate is in the ≥25% zone or not. Report: the share of the single-visit expected regret that arises from first estimates in the zone; regret per 1,000 persons whose first estimate is in the zone and per 1,000 outside it; the single-visit decision disagreement among those in the zone; and the fraction in the zone (check against E11 `targeted_fraction_remeasured`).
2. Exchange-rate sensitivity. The decision rule keeps the guideline threshold p_t; the net benefit is evaluated with an exchange rate p_e ≠ p_t: NB_e(rule) = Σ p(u) (r(u) − p_e)/(1 − p_e) Pr(treat | u). Report, for (p_t, p_e) ∈ {(3%, 2%), (3%, 5%), (5%, 3%), (5%, 7.5%), (10%, 7.5%), (10%, 15%)} for 10-year ASCVD, {(7.5%, 5%), (7.5%, 10%)} for total CVD and {(10%, 7.5%), (10%, 15%)} for 30-year ASCVD: NB_e of the usual-level rule, NB_e of the single-visit rule, their difference per 1,000 (signed; a single-visit decision can be better than the usual-level decision under p_e ≠ p_t), the difference relative to NB_e of the usual-level rule, and the net benefit achievable by the optimal rule at p_e. Single-visit treatment probabilities are the E11 closed form (normal errors, symmetric systematic difference).
3. Interpretation rule: these do not replace the E11 primary; they are reported as sensitivity analyses in the Supplement and summarized in one sentence each in the main text. The bootstrap intervals cover sampling uncertainty under the model only; the main text states that model uncertainty is not included.

## C. Reexamination subsample checks

1. θ by interval between examinations: tertiles of `rr_days` (MXRRDAYS; cut-points from the data; participants with a missing interval reported separately and excluded from the tertiles), CR1 logit-t intervals per tertile, and a trend from a logistic regression of the change indicator on interval/10 days with a CR1 cluster-robust (pseudo-PSU) sandwich variance, reported as an odds ratio per 10 days with a t(G − 1) interval. No causal reading: a longer interval admits real change and seasonal drift.
2. Completeness: of the 979 eligible participants, compare the 759 with complete base PREVENT inputs at both examinations with the 220 without, on age (mean, SD), women, race and ethnicity (non-Hispanic White, non-Hispanic Black, Mexican American, other), current smoking, antihypertensive treatment, self-reported diabetes and statin use (where known); report proportions, means and standardized differences. Reasons for incompleteness are not separately classified (missing laboratory, blood pressure or diabetes-status data at either examination).
3. Statement for the manuscript: the published NHANES III within-person variation values (Lacher 2005; Selvin 2013) were derived from the same second-examination subsample, so their agreement with ours checks the data processing, not the representativeness of the subsample.

## D. Transport of the error variance (model-based scenario grid)

1. Model: the prespecified model A simulation (independent errors around each participant's two-visit mean; SDs from the between-visit differences divided by √2; SBP and HbA1c on the original scale, lipids and creatinine on the log scale; `nh3_posthoc_repeat_strategies.py` implementation, which reproduces θ_sim = 11.4%).
2. Scenarios: laboratory error at the observed 1988–1994 level or at a modern research-protocol level, crossed with the SBP error SD multiplied by 0.75, 1.0, 1.25 and 1.5 (8 scenarios). The modern laboratory level is the total within-person CV = √(CVI² + CVa²) with CVI the frozen pre-freeze transcription of the EFLM meta-analysis (total cholesterol 5.4%, HDL cholesterol 5.7%, creatinine 4.4%, HbA1c 1.2%; `prefreeze_facts_v1.json`) and CVa the observed NHANES III quality-control CVs in the same frozen table (total cholesterol 1.33%, HDL cholesterol 2.49%, creatinine 2.7%, HbA1c 2.0%), which are already at modern levels; the log-scale SD is √ln(1 + CV²); the HbA1c SD is CV × the mean HbA1c of the sample. The SBP multipliers are not estimates of current practice; they span plausible device and protocol effects (the observed SD is 7.3 mm Hg for the mean of two readings).
3. Outputs per scenario, from 200 simulated assessment pairs per participant: θ_sim (two independent assessments disagree); a π_clin-type curve fitted to the pooled simulated pairs with the prespecified specification (restricted cubic spline of the logit distance of the first assessment's risk from the nearest threshold, knots at the 10th/50th/90th percentiles, nearest-threshold indicators) → zones (≥25%, ≥10%; truncated) and peaks; the share of first assessments in the union of the ≥25% zones; and the implied SDs. Seeds 20261009 + 200 + scenario index.
4. Interpretation rule: scenario values are model-based and describe how θ and the zones scale with the error variance under independence; they are not estimates of current practice and are reported with that label (one sentence in the main text; table in the Supplement).

Results file with the protocol hash; rows in `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`; Supplemental Table S39 (A–D) and Supplemental Methods paragraph; main-text sentences per the interpretation rules above; an addendum to this protocol for any departure.

## Addendum 1 (2026-10-11, after the independent code audit of the first run)

The audit reproduced every quantity to machine precision and found no protocol violation. Items fixed before the second run (point estimates unchanged):

1. D3 defined the share of *simulated first assessments* in the ≥25% zones; the first run reported the share of the *observed* first-visit risks in the scenario zones (about 1 pp lower). The second run reports the simulated share (key `share_simulated_first_assessment_in_union_zone`) as the protocol quantity and keeps the observed share as a secondary quantity.
2. Ratio-type intervals (loss fraction of the rule's net benefit; share of regret arising in the zone) silently dropped replicates in which the denominator was undefined (rule net benefit ≤ 0), leaving a one-replicate interval for the 10%-decision/15%-exchange-rate case and an unstable interval for the 30-year 10%/15% case. Rule: a ratio interval is reported only when the point and every replicate are defined; the number of undefined replicates and of replicates with non-positive rule net benefit is stored; the signed loss per 1,000 and its interval remain the stable quantities.
3. Removed `regret_per_1000_in_zone_as_share_of_achievable_per_1000` (mixed denominators; unused).
4. The EFLM CVI values are now asserted against the frozen transcription (`nh3_figures.eflm_cvi`); CVa provenance (pool identities) is recorded in the results file.
5. Added the zone width in logit units (2·d*) beside d*; recorded that limit intervals are percentile intervals among replicates with a non-empty zone and that the 5% ≥10% lower-limit interval is bounded by the truncation rule; asserted that the truncated point zones equal the frozen zones used by the repeat-strategy and E11 analyses.
6. The E11 point-estimate fit is cached for reruns (identical call, identical result).
